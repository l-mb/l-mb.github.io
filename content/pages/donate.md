Today, it is ever more important that we stand together, and support those who 
push for our values.

If you can, please donate; once, or even better regularly. If you cannot: thank 
you for at least thinking about it, and please spread the message.

Here are a few charities for causes I believe in, in alphabetical order (because 
they are all important to me, and so many others, and choosing is hard).

- [Amadeu Antonio Stiftung](https://www.amadeu-antonio-stiftung.de/en/donations-and-endowments/)
- [Amnesty International](https://www.amnesty.org/en/donate/) or [Amnesty International Deutschland](https://helfen.amnesty.de/)
- [Free Software Foundation Europe](https://my.fsfe.org/donate)
- [Gesellschaft für Freiheitsrechte (GFF)](https://freiheitsrechte.org/wehrhafte-demokratie)
- [Hate Aid](https://hateaid.org/spenden/)
- [LSVD](https://www.lsvd.de/de/ct/93-Jetzt-Spenden-und-Menschenrechte-von-LSBTIQ-unterstuetzen)
- [Netzpolitik.org](https://netzpolitik.org/spenden/)
- [Polylux e.V.](https://polylux.network/de/spenden)
- [Pro Asyl](https://www.proasyl.de/spenden/)
- [Seebrücke](https://www.seebruecke.org/spenden)

If any of my writings have inspired you to donate, I'd love for a shout out in 
private or social media. I don't often ask for the latter, but our open 
democracies - such as they are - are under threat, and we need all the help we 
can get.
